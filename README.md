# Nigeria DHS Immunization Analysis

Analysis of childhood immunization coverage, vaccine equity, and determinants of vaccination uptake in Nigeria using Demographic and Health Survey (DHS) data, R programming, and survey-weighted statistical methods.

## Overview

This repository presents an analysis of childhood immunization coverage and determinants of vaccination uptake in Nigeria using nationally representative DHS datasets.

The project examines factors associated with immunization status, including maternal healthcare utilization, socioeconomic characteristics, maternal education, geographic location, and access to health services. Using survey-weighted statistical methods in R, the analysis identifies key drivers of vaccination coverage and highlights inequities affecting child health outcomes.

## Research Objectives

* Assess childhood immunization coverage in Nigeria.
* Identify determinants of vaccination uptake.
* Examine socioeconomic and geographic disparities in immunization.
* Generate evidence to support immunization programs and policy decisions.

## Methods

* Secondary analysis of Nigeria DHS data
* Data cleaning and harmonization
* Survey-weighted descriptive statistics
* Rao-Scott chi-square testing
* Logistic regression analysis
* Data visualization and interpretation

## Key Findings

* Childhood immunization coverage remains uneven across regions of Nigeria.
* Maternal healthcare utilization is strongly associated with vaccination uptake.
* Maternal education and household wealth significantly influence immunization status.
* Geographic and socioeconomic disparities persist despite improvements in vaccine coverage.
* Findings support targeted interventions to improve equitable access to childhood immunization services.
